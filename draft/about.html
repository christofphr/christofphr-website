PROFILE

Originally from Oklahoma, Chris is based in New York City. He’s drawn to warm lighting and places with character: the city, a car, a pool table... rooms where something just happened or is about to.

His work lives in the tension between grit and restraint, balancing between what's been carefully composed and scenes with frayed edges. He photographs with an appreciation for places that feel lived-in, even when they're impeccably curated.
